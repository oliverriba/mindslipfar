# I WATCHED YOU AS YOUR MIND SLIPPED FAR (AWAY FROM ME)

> *"you were right there. and then you weren't."*

A retro-style pixel puzzle game about loss, wandering, and the moment reality comes undone.

---

## 🌲 about the game

You are alone in a dark forest. The trees don't move the way trees should.

Somewhere ahead: a key. Somewhere beyond that: a door. And beyond the door — the sun swallows you whole.

**I WATCHED YOU AS YOUR MIND SLIPPED FAR (AWAY FROM ME)** is a short, atmospheric pixel puzzle game built in Pico-8. It is quiet until it isn't.

---

## 🎮 gameplay

- Explore a dark retro forest
- Find the **key**
- Find the **door**
- Enter
- Fall

The puzzle isn't just about finding objects — it's about *what you notice along the way.*
