# design notes — I WATCHED YOU AS YOUR MIND SLIPPED FAR (AWAY FROM ME)

---

## core vision

This is a short game. Maybe 5–15 minutes. It doesn't need to be long to land.

The feeling we're after: *dread that grows slowly, then breaks open all at once.*

The forest should feel wrong from the start — not jump-scare wrong, but **off**. Colors slightly too dark. Trees that repeat a little too often. Music that almost resolves but doesn't.

Then the door. Then the fall. Then the sun — and everything that means.

---

## player experience arc

```
CALM (uneasy) → SEARCHING → FOUND (relief?) → DOOR → FREEFALL → OVERWHELM → SILENCE
```

---

## mechanics

### movement
- Simple 4-directional grid movement (tile-based)
- Slow, deliberate. No running.

### the forest
- Dark palette — deep greens, blacks, maybe one or two muted purples
- Tile-based map, hand-designed
- Some tiles are "wrong" — interact with them and get a line of text
- The forest loops slightly (player may not notice at first)

### the key
- Sitting somewhere in the forest, glowing faintly
- Simple pickup — walk over it
- Once picked up, something in the forest changes (a sound, a shift in color)

### the door
- Only appears (or becomes visible) once the key is found
- Interacting with the door triggers the transition

### the fall / sun sequence
- Cuts to a different visual mode — no more tile grid
- Player "falls" downward while surreal imagery scrolls past
- The sun grows larger
- Strange text appears — fragmented, emotional
- Screen whites out
- Short final message. Then silence.

---

## visual style

- Pico-8 palette (16 colors) — lean on the darker half
- 8x8 sprites, tile-based world
- The sun sequence can break the rules — glitchy, chaotic, overwhelming
- Consider screen shake, flashing colors (add a seizure warning if so)

---

## audio

- Ambient forest loop: low drones, distant sounds, minimal melody
- Key pickup: a soft, unresolved musical moment
- Door: silence just before
- Fall sequence: builds into noise, then cuts

---

## tone reference points

*(not copying, just feeling)*
- Yume Nikki (dreamlike exploration, quiet dread)
- OFF (surreal, emotional, retro)
- Undertale's quieter moments
- Old Flash games that felt like they meant something

---

## what the game is actually about

Up to you. But the title suggests watching someone you love change into someone unreachable — through mental illness, addiction, grief, distance, time.

The forest is the distance. The key is the hope you keep. The door is the moment you finally let yourself feel it. The fall into the sun is what that actually feels like inside.

Or it's something else entirely. That's okay too.
