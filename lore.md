# lore & world — I WATCHED YOU AS YOUR MIND SLIPPED FAR (AWAY FROM ME)

---

## the forest

The forest has no name. It may not be a real forest.

It is the kind of place you end up when you've been circling something painful for too long — familiar enough to walk through, strange enough to never feel safe.

The trees are tall. They absorb sound. They have been here longer than whatever this is about.

---

## the key

Small. Brass, maybe. Or something that looks like light caught in a shape.

You don't know what it opens. You take it anyway. That's what you do — you keep collecting small hopes.

---

## the door

It was always there. You just couldn't see it until you were ready.

Or: it wasn't there, and now it is. The forest made it for you.

Either way, it opens.

---

## the sun

The sun is not a metaphor. The sun is the sun.

It is enormous and warm and it has been waiting. Falling into it is not death. It might be the opposite.

Or it's just overwhelming — the way some feelings are so big they become light, become heat, become everything at once and then nothing, and then you're sitting somewhere quiet wondering what just happened.
